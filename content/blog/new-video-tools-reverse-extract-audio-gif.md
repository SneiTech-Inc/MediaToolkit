## Reverse Video: Play It Backwards

Ever wanted to see your video run in reverse? Whether you're creating a dramatic slow-motion effect, making a funny reversed clip, or experimenting with creative storytelling, the Reverse Video tool is your answer.

Upload your video, and our advanced video processing engine will play it backwards — from the last frame to the first. The audio is also reversed, keeping everything in perfect sync.

## Extract Audio: Turn Videos into Soundtracks

Sometimes you only need the audio from a video — a song from a music video, a voiceover from a tutorial, or background music from a clip. The Extract Audio tool lets you pull out the audio and save it in the format you need.

### Supported Formats
You can extract audio as:
- **MP3** (best for general use, with bitrate control)
- **WAV** (high quality, uncompressed)
- **AAC** (Apple‑friendly, great for mobile)
- **OGG** (open format, good quality)
- **FLAC** (lossless, perfect for archiving)
- **M4A** (universal, works on all devices)

### Quality Controls
Each format offers its own quality settings:
- **MP3/AAC/M4A**: Adjust the bitrate (64–320 kbps) to balance file size and audio quality.
- **WAV**: Choose sample rate (44.1 kHz or 48 kHz).
- **OGG**: Set a quality level (0–10) for variable bitrate.
- **FLAC**: Choose compression level (0–8) to trade processing time for file size.

### How to Extract Audio
1. Go to the [Extract Audio](/tools/video/extract-audio) tool.
2. Upload your video.
3. Select your desired audio format.
4. Choose quality settings.
5. Click "Extract Audio" and download your file.

## Video to GIF: Create Animated GIFs

GIFs are everywhere — social media, messaging apps, memes. Our Video to GIF converter lets you turn any video clip into a high-quality animated GIF.

### What You Can Customize
- **Segment selection**: Pick the exact start time and duration.
- **Resolution**: Set the width and height (aspect ratio is automatically maintained).
- **Frame rate**: Choose from 5 to 30 frames per second (higher = smoother, larger file).
- **Quality**: Low, Medium, or High — controls the colour palette and file size.
- **Looping**: Choose how many times the GIF loops (0 = infinite).

### How to Create a GIF
1. Go to the [Video to GIF](/tools/video/video-to-gif) tool.
2. Upload your video.
3. Select the segment you want (start time and duration).
4. Adjust settings like width, frame rate, and quality.
5. Click "Convert to GIF" and download your animated masterpiece.
