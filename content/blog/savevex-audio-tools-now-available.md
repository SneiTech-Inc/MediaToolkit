# SaveVex Audio Tools: Now Available — Convert, Merge, Trim, and Boost Your Audio

We're excited to announce the launch of **SaveVex Audio Tools** — a suite of four free, browser-based audio processing tools designed for creators, podcasters, musicians, and anyone who works with sound. Whether you need to convert formats, combine tracks, extract a perfect clip, or adjust volume levels, the Audio Tools have you covered.

Audio Tools join our growing lineup of PDF, Image, Document, Text, and Utility tools, making SaveVex the most comprehensive free media toolkit available — all without leaving your browser and without a single upload to a remote server.

## What Are Audio Tools?

Audio files come in many formats, and working with them often means juggling multiple applications. Podcasters need to trim interviews. Musicians need to convert between formats. Content creators need to merge background music with voiceovers. Everyday users just want to make a recording louder before sharing it.

SaveVex Audio Tools handle all of these tasks directly in your browser. Every tool uses advanced audio processing technology that runs locally on your device. Your files are never uploaded, never stored remotely, and never shared. Start the tool, load your file, get your result — it's that simple.

## Meet the Tools

Here's a tour of all four Audio Tools — each built to solve a specific audio challenge.

### 🔄 Convert Audio

Need to switch between audio formats? Convert Audio supports **MP3, WAV, AAC, OGG, FLAC, and M4A** — any format to any other format. Choose your target format and quality settings (bitrate, sample rate, or compression level depending on the format), and convert in seconds. Perfect for making files compatible with different devices, apps, or platforms.

**[Try Convert Audio →](/tools/audio/convert-audio)**

### 🔗 Merge Audio

Combining multiple audio files into a single track has never been easier. Upload up to 20 files in any supported format — even mixing different formats in the same merge — and arrange them in the order you want by dragging and dropping. Merge Audio automatically normalizes every track to the same sample rate and channel layout, so the output plays back smoothly without glitches or gaps.

**[Try Merge Audio →](/tools/audio/merge-audio)**

### ✂️ Trim Audio

Extract the exact segment you need from any audio file. Drag the dual-handle slider or type precise start and end times to select your segment, then preview it with the built-in player before exporting. Whether you're cutting a highlight from a long recording, trimming silence from the start or end, or pulling a clip for a project, Trim Audio makes it effortless.

**[Try Trim Audio →](/tools/audio/trim-audio)**

### 🔊 Change Volume

Adjust the loudness of any audio file — reduce background music, boost a quiet recording, or normalize to a standard level. The real-time preview lets you hear the volume change instantly (no re-encoding needed), while the percent/dB toggle gives you precise control. Preset buttons for Mute, 25%, 50%, Normal, 150%, and 200% make common adjustments one click away. When boosting, a built-in limiter prevents clipping and distortion — even on already-loud source material.

**[Try Change Volume →](/tools/audio/change-volume)**

## How to Use Audio Tools

All Audio Tools are **completely free** and require **no sign-up or account**. Just navigate to the tool you need, upload your file, configure your settings, and download the result.

Privacy is fundamental to how SaveVex works. Every Audio Tool processes files locally in your browser using advanced audio processing technology. Your recordings, tracks, and mixes never leave your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Nothing is stored remotely. Nothing is tracked. This means your files are always 100% private and secure.

Find all Audio Tools under the **Audio** category in the site navigation, or visit the [Audio Tools page](/tools/audio) to browse the full collection.
