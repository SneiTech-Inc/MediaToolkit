# Meet Sneitech Inc. — The Company Behind SaveVex

SaveVex didn't start with a pitch deck or a funding round. It started on a Friday afternoon in early 2024 when I needed to merge two PDFs and couldn't find a free tool I trusted. Every option either wanted me to upload my files to a server I knew nothing about, pay a subscription for a task I'd do once a month, or install software that came bundled with things I didn't want. I had been building software professionally for over a decade, so I decided to solve the problem myself. That decision grew into SaveVex — and SaveVex grew into the flagship product of Sneitech Inc.

**Sneitech Inc.** is the company I founded to build software that respects people. We're a product-development firm based in Ghana, serving users worldwide with tools designed around a simple principle: your data is yours, and the best way to protect it is to never collect it in the first place.

## What We Do

Sneitech Inc. builds digital products — web applications, mobile apps, and cloud-based tools — for businesses and communities. Our primary focus is on privacy-first software: products that deliver real utility without harvesting user data, running intrusive tracking, or locking essential features behind paywalls.

SaveVex is our most visible product, but it represents a philosophy that drives everything we build. Every line of code, every feature decision, and every product we ship starts from the same question: "Would I feel comfortable using this with my own data?" If the answer is no, we don't ship it.

Beyond our own products, we provide custom software development for select clients — building web platforms, mobile applications, and cloud infrastructure with the same commitment to quality and privacy that defines SaveVex.

## Our Values

**Privacy.** Your data belongs to you. This isn't a marketing line — it's the engineering constraint that shapes every technical decision we make. SaveVex processes files entirely in your browser because that's the only way to guarantee your files never end up on a server you didn't choose. No workarounds, no exceptions.

**Simplicity.** Tools should be easy to use. We spend as much time on interface design as we do on backend engineering because a powerful tool that's confusing to use might as well not exist. Every feature on SaveVex passes a simple test: can someone who's never used the tool before figure it out in under 30 seconds?

**Reliability.** Software that works when you need it. We test across browsers, devices, and file types because real users have real deadlines. A PDF merger that crashes on large files isn't a tool — it's a liability. We ship when things are ready, not when a calendar says we should.

**Community.** We build tools that help people and businesses grow. SaveVex is free because we believe access to professional-grade file processing shouldn't depend on your budget. The feedback, bug reports, and feature requests from our users shape what we build next. Every tool on the platform exists because someone genuinely needed it.

## Our Team

Sneitech Inc. is a small, focused team based in Ghana. I founded the company and serve as CEO, leading product design and engineering. I've spent over 10 years building software across the full stack — .NET, Next.js, React, cloud infrastructure, and file processing technologies — and I've personally designed and built every tool on SaveVex.

We operate lean by choice. A small team means faster decisions, cleaner code, and no layers of management between a user's feedback and the person who can act on it. When someone reports a bug or suggests a feature, it reaches the right person within hours, not weeks.

Our location in Ghana is intentional. There's incredible engineering talent across Africa that the global tech industry consistently overlooks, and we're building a company that proves what's possible when you invest in that talent. We're also deeply connected to the communities we serve — we understand the needs of users who don't have reliable high-speed internet, who work primarily on mobile devices, and who can't afford expensive software subscriptions. Those perspectives shape our products in ways that Silicon Valley would miss.

## The SaveVex Story

SaveVex started, as I mentioned, with two PDFs and a Friday afternoon. The first version was a single-page web app that merged PDFs using nothing but browser APIs. I shared it with a few friends, they shared it with their colleagues, and within a month we had a few thousand users. The feedback was consistent: "This is great — can it also split PDFs? Compress images? Convert videos?"

So I kept building. Each new tool followed the same constraints: no uploads, no accounts, no fees, no compromises on privacy. Today SaveVex has over 60 tools across seven categories — PDF, images, video, audio, documents, text, and utilities — serving users in more than 120 countries. And we're just getting started. A mobile app with on-device AI features is in development, and we have plans for more tools, more capabilities, and more ways to make powerful file processing accessible to everyone.

Why is SaveVex free? Because the internet already has enough products that bait you with "free" and then lock the useful features behind a subscription. We'd rather build something genuinely valuable, give it away, and trust that the people who can afford to support us will. Our [Premium page](/premium) offers optional support options for those who want to help keep the servers running and the tools improving. No pressure, no paywalls — just a way for the community to invest in something they find useful.
