# Why Support SaveVex? — A Personal Invitation

When I started building SaveVex, I had one simple goal: create a free, private file toolkit that anyone could use, anywhere in the world, without worrying about sign-ups, hidden costs, or their data being sold. No bait-and-switch. No "free trial" that suddenly asks for your credit card. Just tools that work.

I'm proud to say we've done that. In a few short months, SaveVex has grown to over 50 tools — PDF editing, image processing, video conversion, audio tools, text utilities, and more. Every single one is free. Every single one runs entirely in your browser, which means your files never touch a server.

But here's the part nobody tells you about building something free: keeping it free isn't free.

## The Story Behind SaveVex

I built SaveVex because I was tired of the alternatives. You know the ones — the sites that promise a free PDF compressor, then watermark your document. The converters that work great for two files, then lock you behind a paywall. The "free" tools that quietly upload your files to who-knows-where and probably sell your data to who-knows-who.

I thought: browsers are powerful now. WebAssembly, the File API, modern JavaScript engines — they can handle real work locally. So why isn't anyone building tools that respect both your privacy and your wallet?

That question turned into SaveVex. What started as a handful of PDF tools grew into something much bigger. Image compression, video trimming, audio conversion, QR code generation, password generators, hash tools — the list kept growing because users kept asking for more. And honestly? I loved building it.

But here's the reality: every tool on SaveVex runs entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device. This is the right way to build software — but it also means we can't show ads while you're processing files (we don't want to interrupt your workflow), and we don't sell your data (we never have, and we never will). Those are two of the most common ways free tools make money, and we've chosen neither.

## What Your Support Does

When you support SaveVex, you're not just paying for server costs — you're investing in a vision of the internet where powerful tools are accessible to everyone, regardless of their budget. Here's exactly where your contributions go:

- **Server costs:** Hosting the website, handling traffic, and keeping everything fast and available around the world.
- **Development time:** Every new tool, every bug fix, every performance improvement — that's hours of careful work by real people. Your support lets us keep building instead of burning out.
- **AI infrastructure:** The upcoming AI-powered tools — video generation, image creation, background removal, and more — require real compute resources. Even when processing happens locally, the models themselves need to be hosted and served.
- **Keeping it free:** Your support is the reason we can say "free for everyone, forever" and actually mean it. Without it, we'd eventually have to make compromises we don't want to make.

## A Growing Community

SaveVex isn't built by a big corporation. It's built by a small team — and increasingly, by a community of people who believe in what we're doing. Every email we get, every suggestion for a new tool, every person who shares SaveVex with a friend — that's what keeps this project alive.

We've had users from over 80 countries use SaveVex. Students who can't afford expensive software subscriptions. Freelancers who need quick file conversions without trusting sketchy websites. Developers who appreciate tools that don't phone home. That's who we're building for.

Your support — whether it's financial or simply spreading the word — is what makes this sustainable. It turns SaveVex from a side project that might eventually fizzle out into something that can grow and improve for years.

## What's Coming Next

With your support, we're already building some exciting things. The SaveVex Mobile App is in development, bringing AI-powered features like video generation, image creation, and cartoon-style filters to your phone. We're also planning new browser-based tools, a smoother user experience, and even deeper privacy protections.

But we can't build it alone. Every contribution accelerates development. Every supporter gives us the confidence to invest more time and resources into making SaveVex better for everyone.
