# SaveVex Utility Tools: Now Available — Everything You Need in One Place

We're thrilled to announce the launch of **SaveVex Utility Tools** — a collection of seven free, browser-based tools designed to make everyday tasks faster, easier, and completely private. Whether you're a developer debugging timestamps, a designer picking color palettes, or just someone who needs a quick QR code, the Utility Tools have you covered.

These tools join our existing lineup of PDF, Image, Document, and Text tools, making SaveVex your go-to destination for file processing and productivity — all without leaving your browser.

## What Are Utility Tools?

Utility Tools are the everyday helpers that don't fit neatly into a single file-processing category but are essential to modern workflows. They handle the small-but-critical tasks that developers, designers, content creators, and everyday users encounter constantly.

The best part? Like everything on SaveVex, these tools run **entirely in your browser**. No uploads, no accounts, no waiting for a server to respond. Just instant results with complete privacy.

## Meet the Tools

Here's a tour of all seven Utility Tools — each one built to solve a specific problem elegantly.

### 📲 QR Code Generator

Need a QR code? Generate custom QR codes from URLs, text, or any data in seconds. Upload a logo to brand your QR code, choose custom colors, and download as PNG, SVG, or PDF. Perfect for marketing materials, business cards, event signage, or sharing WiFi credentials.

**[Try QR Code Generator →](/tools/utility/qr-generator)**

### 🎨 Color Picker

Pick any color and instantly see its values in HEX, RGB, HSL, and CMYK — all four formats at once. Explore complementary, analogous, and triadic color harmonies, or generate a five-color monochromatic palette from your base color. Recently picked colors are saved locally for quick access. An essential tool for designers and front-end developers.

**[Try Color Picker →](/tools/utility/color-picker)**

### ⏰ Timestamp Converter

Working with Unix timestamps and need to know what date they represent? Or need to convert a date back to a timestamp? The Timestamp Converter handles both directions — with auto-detection of seconds vs milliseconds, UTC and local timezone support, and multiple output formats. The live-updating current timestamp in the sidebar is perfect for quick copy-paste operations.

**[Try Timestamp Converter →](/tools/utility/timestamp-converter)**

### 🆔 UUID Generator

Generate universally unique identifiers in v1, v4, v6, and v7 formats. Need bulk UUIDs? Generate up to 100 at once. Copy individual IDs, copy all at once, or download as a .txt file. Version 7 UUIDs are timestamp-ordered, making them ideal for database primary keys.

**[Try UUID Generator →](/tools/utility/uuid-generator)**

### 🔏 Hash Generator

Generate cryptographic hashes using MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-384, and SHA-512. Paste text or upload a .txt file, and all selected hashes compute simultaneously. Compare outputs side by side, copy individual hashes, or download all results as a formatted file. Great for verifying file integrity, generating checksums, or learning about hash functions.

**[Try Hash Generator →](/tools/utility/hash-generator)**

### 📝 Lorem Ipsum Generator

Need placeholder text for a design mockup, website prototype, or document layout? Generate Lorem Ipsum text in four modes: paragraphs, sentences, words, or characters. Use the presets (Short, Medium, Long, Very Long) for quick generation, or fine-tune the count with the slider. Optionally prefix the text with a custom starting phrase.

**[Try Lorem Ipsum Generator →](/tools/utility/lorem-ipsum-generator)**

### 🔑 Password Generator

Create strong, random passwords with full control over length (4–64 characters), character types (uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols), and optional exclusions for ambiguous or duplicate characters. Each password shows its strength rating and entropy estimate, so you know exactly how secure it is. And yes — passwords are generated locally and never stored or transmitted.

**[Try Password Generator →](/tools/utility/password-generator)**

## How to Use Utility Tools

All Utility Tools are **completely free** and require **no sign-up or account**. Just navigate to the tool you need, start using it, and get your results instantly.

Privacy is baked into everything we build. Every tool processes data locally in your browser using secure, built-in browser capabilities. Your text, colors, timestamps, hashes, and passwords never leave your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Nothing is stored remotely. Nothing is tracked.

You can find all Utility Tools under the **Utility** category in the site navigation, or visit the [Utility Tools page](/tools/utility) to browse the full collection.
