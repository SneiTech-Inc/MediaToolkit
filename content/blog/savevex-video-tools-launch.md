# SaveVex Video Tools: Now Available — Compress, Convert, Trim, Merge, Crop, Rotate, Resize, and Speed Control

We're excited to announce that all eight of SaveVex's video tools are now live and ready for you to use. Whether you're a content creator, educator, marketer, or just someone who needs to do a quick video edit, these tools give you everything you need — completely free, with no sign-up required, and all processing happens right in your browser.

## What's New: The Complete Video Toolkit

Our video toolkit covers every common editing task you'd need, without downloading any software or uploading your files to a server. Here's the full lineup:

### 🎬 Compress Video
Need to shrink a video file for email, social media, or storage? [Compress Video](/tools/video/compress-video) reduces file sizes while keeping quality high. Perfect for large recordings, long presentations, or any video that's just too big to share.

### 🔄 Convert Video
Got a video in the wrong format? [Convert Video](/tools/video/convert-video) switches between MP4, MOV, AVI, and MKV in seconds. Whether your device only plays certain formats or you need a specific container for editing software, we've got you covered.

### ✂️ Trim Video
Extract the perfect clip from a longer recording. [Trim Video](/tools/video/trim-video) lets you select start and end points with precision — use the visual timeline, type exact timestamps, or step frame-by-frame for pixel-perfect cuts.

### 🔗 Merge Video
Combine multiple clips into one seamless video. [Merge Video](/tools/video/merge-video) supports drag-and-drop reordering so you can arrange your clips in any order, then merge them into a single file in seconds.

### 🎯 Crop Video
Remove unwanted edges, reframe your shot, or crop to a specific aspect ratio. [Crop Video](/tools/video/crop-video) offers interactive drag-to-select cropping, aspect ratio presets (1:1, 4:3, 16:9, and more), and manual pixel-precise controls.

### 🔁 Rotate Video
Fix sideways footage or get creative with flips. [Rotate Video](/tools/video/rotate-video) offers quick 90° turns, a 0-360° slider for custom angles, and independent horizontal and vertical flip toggles. The live preview shows exactly what your output will look like before you process.

### 📐 Resize Video
Change your video's resolution — scale down for faster sharing or up for a consistent look across clips. [Resize Video](/tools/video/resize-video) offers orientation-aware presets (portrait videos stay portrait), custom dimensions with aspect ratio lock, and two scale modes: Fit (letterbox) and Fill (crop).

### ⚡ Video Speed Controller
Create slow-motion effects, speed through long lectures, or adjust playback tempo. [Video Speed Controller](/tools/video/video-speed) lets you choose any speed from 0.25× to 4× with a simple slider. The audio pitch stays natural at every speed — no chipmunk voices or deep rumbles.

## New Additions: Reverse Video, Extract Audio, and Video to GIF

We've since added **three more** video tools to complete the set:

- **Reverse Video** — Play your videos backwards for creative effects. The audio reverses too, staying perfectly in sync. Perfect for surreal effects, funny compilations, and creative storytelling.
- **Extract Audio** — Save audio from video files as MP3, WAV, AAC, OGG, FLAC, or M4A. Each format has its own quality controls so you get exactly the output you need.
- **Video to GIF** — Convert any video clip into an animated GIF. Customize the segment, resolution, frame rate, quality, and loop settings for the perfect result.
